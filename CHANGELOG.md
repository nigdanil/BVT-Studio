# Changelog

## 0.4.0-alpha.5-demo.2 — 2026-11-22

- First public experimental demo: 13 ready-to-open Blender scenes and four release ZIPs.
- Add macro droplets, microbeads, running water and combined optical profiles, independent glass targets and JSON presets.
- Calibrate the seven zero-water comparisons against matched neutral atlases.
- Add self-contained example resources, release builder and source CI validation.
- Preserve packed precision masks and float OpenEXR condensation atlases across Blender reload; normalize packed image paths.
- Validated baseline: 193 tests, eight post-merge Blender integration smokes, 13 scene builds/reopens and 13 isolated rerenders on Windows Blender 5.2.3.
- Experimental synthetic fixture only; real-world accuracy and physical realism remain unvalidated.

## 0.4.0-alpha.5-step5 — 2026-11-18

- Add target-matched optical Quality Gate v2 with distinct Light / Medium / Heavy
  synthetic atlas densities and streak amounts. Provisional intensity bands
  replace Step 4's unconditional reward for stronger optical differences.
- Add sensitive bright-contact-rim, local SKU gradient suppression, mean colour
  drift, precision-mask leakage metrics, and explicit per-glass isolation QA.
- Preserve read-only YOLO Detect TXT as SKU ROI, verify SHA256 after QA; otherwise
  mark approximate ROI provenance. No YOLO or dataset-export source changes.
- Add Blender 3x3 optical comparison, 2x2 selected-profile montage, JSON report,
  packed last-profile `.blend` persistence smoke and sidebar report support.
- Metrics are numerical proxies and masks use a synthetic QA fixture; neither
  constitutes proof of OCR quality or photorealistic water.

## 0.4.0-alpha.5-step2 — 2026-11-18

- Add `REALISTIC_ATLAS` as a third independent glass droplet source: procedurally
  generated multi-scale Alpha/Height/OpenGL-Normal PNG triplets (synthetic, **not photographs**).
- Generate irregular lens-cap beads, dense micro-droplets and downward trails with
  explicit Seed, Resolution, Density and Streaks controls in the Blender sidebar.
- Pack generated atlas maps into `.blend` per glass without overwriting imported
  `PHOTO_MAPS`; keep `PROCEDURAL` available and Precision Mask final gating unchanged.
- Add three-mode Blender visual comparison, PNG atlas previews and packed-map
  persistence checks. No changes to YOLO annotation or export modules.
