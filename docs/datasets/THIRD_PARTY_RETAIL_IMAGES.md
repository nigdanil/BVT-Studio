## 3. What BVT-Studio may do under these license terms

Subject to confirming the source-page statements, permissions, other rights, and local law, the indicated Creative Commons terms generally allow:

1. Keeping original photographs as source inputs for internal tests and reproducible QA.
2. Applying simulated condensation, frost, droplets, running water, dirt, optical distortion, reflections, or blur as *derivative image outputs*.
3. Creating and reviewing masks, detection boxes, and annotations associated with the source images.
4. Reporting quantitative comparisons of source and modified imagery, including detector performance and image-quality metrics.
5. Publishing or commercially using photographs and derivatives **when the license-specific conditions below are met**.

These permissions do not establish that BVT-Studio has verified the image annotations, the glass geometry, a model's real-world accuracy, or rights in all third-party elements shown in a scene.

### 3.1 CC0 1.0

CC0 is the lowest-friction subset for re-use and public demos. Copyright attribution is generally not required by CC0, but crediting the source file and author (where available) remains recommended for research provenance. **CC0 does not remove unrelated trademark, privacy, personality, or other third-party rights.**

### 3.2 CC BY 2.0 / CC BY 4.0

Re-use, modification, commercial applications, and publication are permitted under their respective license terms. Retain the creator credit and relevant notices, give the source and license links, identify changes made, and avoid suggesting endorsement by the photographer or Wikimedia Commons. Apply the exact version stated on the source page.

### 3.3 CC BY-SA 2.5 / 3.0 / 4.0

Re-use and modification, including commercial use, are permitted under the applicable version, **but public distribution of adapted photographs is subject to ShareAlike**. Credit the author and source, link the original license, identify modifications, and license the *adaptation* under the same or a legally permitted compatible license for that particular version. Do not assume all BY-SA versions or other licenses are interchangeable; review the specific legal code when publishing.

**Practical distinction:** ShareAlike can apply to the distributed modified photograph without automatically relicensing independent BVT-Studio source code, numerical evaluation results, or unrelated original assets. For mixed bundles, keep the license associated with each individual asset.

## 4. GitHub publication policy

**Publication of original or modified photographs on GitHub is conditional**, not automatic merely because a file is listed in this register:

- Publish only assets whose source-page license, author/credit, copyright notices, and third-party-rights issues have been checked.
- Preserve the source-to-derivative lineage and record all artifact operations.
- Keep the original image license associated with each photo; do not mark the entire Commons collection `GPL-3.0`, `CC0`, or a single blanket Creative Commons license.
- Where a CC BY-SA photograph is modified and publicly shared, attach a compliant ShareAlike license declaration to that specific derivative and provide the required attribution.
- For CC BY photographs, provide full credit, the relevant license link, the source URL, and a statement that the image was modified.
- For CC0 photographs, retain provenance even though copyright attribution is not normally required.
- Do not publish bulk customer/private datasets, images containing sensitive information, or unreviewed third-party content.
- Avoid committing a large photograph collection directly into the code repository unless necessary; a documented data release with per-file manifests and checksums may be more maintainable.

The [BVT-Studio code license](https://github.com/nigdanil/BVT-Studio/blob/main/LICENSE) is **GNU GPLv3**. The source-code license does not itself determine the license of separately sourced photos. GitHub hosting does not override a photographer's license or automatically make every image usable for every downstream purpose.

## 6. BVT-Studio image quality and model evaluation

- **Originals remain unmodified:** preserve the source file bytes for audits and baseline evaluations.
- **Glass masks are scene-specific:** never treat a mask from a synthetic four-pane test fixture as verified on a real Commons photograph.
- **Artifacts stay within verified target regions:** protect faces, signs, product geometry, and other non-glass areas unless an intentional and licensed transformation is being studied.
- **Baseline conditions are documented:** a Commons photograph may already contain reflections, glare, condensation, or blur, so the unaugmented image is not necessarily optically clean.
- **Annotations are not assumed:** these 100 registry entries do not establish the existence of YOLO bounding boxes, segmentation masks, class IDs, or SKU-level ground truth.
- **Paired comparisons use aligned dimensions and geometry:** avoid rescaling/cropping between original and derivative if a pixelwise QA gate assumes the same coordinate grid.
- **No train/test leakage:** variants of the same original must stay within one dataset split; use independent real-world imagery for validation where applicable.
- **Be precise about findings:** report dataset size actually analyzed, model/version, parameters, license provenance, mAP/precision/recall and their limits; do not claim detector improvement from render QA alone.
